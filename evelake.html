Study 07

King Evelake

On the eve of a great battle with King Tholomer (Tollome), James (Joseph of Arimathea) and Josephus, his son or some say his nephew, went to King Evelake. James informed him that he would be killed in battle by Tholomer (Tollome), unless he left the belief of his old pagan law and believed upon the new law. King Evelake was shown the symbols of the new law and was converted right away, and the Shield of the Most Worthy, with the symbols of the new law, was presented to him, with which he overcame his enemy. After the battle James baptized him.

Not long afterward King Evelake and his brother-in-law Nascien travelled to England to release James and his son Josephus from prison there. It has been said that King Evelake was the first person to have brought Christianity to England with him.

Bloodline of the Holy Grail. Laurence Gardner. Element Books, England 1995. Page 142.

Bishop Josephus informed King Evelake that he would inevitably be killed by Tolleme unless he left his belief of the old law and believed upon the new law. Evelake was immediately converted, and the Shield of the Most Worthy was presented to him, whereupon he defeated Tolleme.

King Arthur and His Noble Knights. Mary MacLeod. Wells Gardner. London. 1908. Page 216.

At the end of the war King Evelake was baptized.

The Holy Grail. Malcolm Godwin. Bloomsbury Ltd. 1994. Page 120.

Josephus and his father travelled to Britain where they were promptly imprisoned. Mordrian, [Evelake] with his brother-in-law Nascien, sailed from the Holy Lands and released them, and brought Christianity to Britain.
