Study 05

Joseph of Arimathea

It was not long after Joseph of Arimathea (James) arrived in England that he fell sick and was about to die. King Evelake asked Joseph (James) to leave a remembrance for them.

Then Joseph asked for the Shield to be brought to him and said, “you shall never see this Shield but you shall think of me,” and that no one will be able to obtain it, as it can cause death, hurt and maim forever, until the time that the Most Worthy Knight, the Good Knight, the Finest Knight, the one that is called to it by name, the last of my lineage, shall come and obtain it.

King Arthur and His Noble Knights. Mary MacLeod. Wells Gardner. London. 1908. Page 217.

Not long after, Joseph of Arimathea fell ill, and was like to die. King Evelake was deeply grieved, and prayed him to leave some token of remembrance, and he bade him bring the Shield.

“Now you may see a remembrance that I love you,” he said, “for you shall never see this Shield but you shall think of me,” and never shall any man bear this Shield around his neck but shall repent it, until the time that Galahad the good knight bear it, and the last of my lineage shall have it about his neck.

The Holy Grail. Malcolm Godwin. Bloomsbury Ltd. 1994. Page 120.

Josephus left the Shield in Britain for the time the Perfect Knight would come.
