Study 09

Alchemy and Magic

All things appear as symbols to the eye. Symbols are the oldest way to communicate with the masses of the people. Pictures can be conveyed to the laity without much education. They were generally frightened by some symbols, as they perceived some as magical symbols.

Universal analogies are the essence of magic.

There is a correspondence, and there are links in the occult, between what seem to be dissimilar things.

Analogies, opposites, symbolism, pictures, laity, initiates. All had to understand the correspondence of seemingly different things, equals but opposites.

Magic Symbols. Frederick Goodman. Brian Todd Publishing. 1989. Page 57.

Duality of symbolism is not accidental. It is designed to point to the Truth, proclaimed by almost all occult and magical systems.

Transcendental Magic. Eliphas Levi. Translated by A.E. Waite. Page 372.

Magic is the science of universal analogies.

Encyclopedia of Forbidden Knowledge. Cassiel. Hamlyn 1990. Page 55.

Correspondence: there are occult links between seemingly dissimilar things.
