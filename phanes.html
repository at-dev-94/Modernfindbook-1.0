Study 04

Grecian Phanes

Phanes is called the Intelligible Light, as well as the Limit or Boundary. Phanes was also called Eros or Love. It is the Light or Boundary because Phanes is visible and seeable. The Revealed One, the Word, the Logos.

Phaen(o) — shining, show, appear.

(PHANES) “LIGHT” was the very first thing to appear. It is said by Orpheus that the Father of all the Gods was PHANES.

Dictionary of all Scriptures and Myths. G.A. Gaskell. Gramercy Books 1960. Pages 243 and 571.

The first to appear is “LIGHT” (PHANES). Orpheus tells us that PHANES is the Father of all the Gods. Phanes was also called Eros, also the Limit or Boundary, also Intelligible Light, and the Light of Truth.

Isis Unveiled. H.P. Blavatsky. Theosophical University Press 1976. Pages 146–7.

It was the God Phanes, the Revealed One, the Word, or Logos.

Shorter Oxford Dictionary on Historical Principles. Clarendon Press 1973. Page 1568.

Phen-Pheno — a formative element in chemistry, from phaen(o): shining, show, appear.
