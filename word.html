Study 01

The Word, the Name, the Meaning

In the Hebrew language these four letters became the Tetragrammaton, YHWH, the Lord (Ba'al) of the North, or (Ba'al) of Lebanon, also called (Ba'al) Tsaphon. This name can be interpreted as Lord of the North. It was never used as a name proper. (Ba'al) was only used to hide the true name of God. (Ba'al) became as popular as the images of Jupiter.

Y.H.W.H. (Yahweh) is comprised of four letters, which mean “that which was, that which is, that which shall be.”

The name of God of the Hebrews really is complete in only three letters. The fourth letter is a copy of the second letter.

The Jews say that the right pronunciation of the name of God does exist on Earth, and it is one of the mysteries that the Messiah will reveal.

That which Was, That which Is, That which shall Be, reminds me of From, As Far As, Until.

New Larousse Encyclopedia of Mythology. Hamlyn. London. 1959. Page 75.

Ba'al, in the Ras Shamrah text, is the same god as Ba'al Tsaphon.

The Mystical Tarot. Rosemary Ellen Guiley. Penguin 1991. Page 18.

YHVH (Yahweh), the Tetragrammaton, comprised of the letters Yod, Heh, Vau, Heh. The Tetragrammaton translates to “that which was, that which is, that which shall be.”

Transcendental Magic. Eliphas Levi. Samuel Weiser, Inc. 1972. Page 45.

The Hebrew name of God is complete in three letters, as the fourth duplicates the second.

Dictionary of Freemasonry. Robert Macoy. Bell Publishing. 1989. Page 684.

The Jews are quite aware that the true pronunciation of the word is lost and regard it as one of the mysteries to be revealed in the days of the Messiah. They hold, however, that the knowledge of the name of God does exist on Earth.
